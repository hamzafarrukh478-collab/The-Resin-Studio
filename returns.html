Our Returns Policy

Important information about returns, damaged items, personalised creations and exchanges.

Before You Order

Please read carefully.

Because our products are handmade, every order is prepared with individual attention.

Our return process depends on the type of product and the reason for the return.

Last updated: August 2026

Can I Return An Item?

If you receive an item that is damaged, incorrect or significantly different from what was ordered, please contact The Resin Studio as soon as possible.

We will review the situation and determine the appropriate solution.

Damaged Items

If your order arrives damaged, contact us promptly with your order details and clear photographs showing the condition of the item and packaging.

Please keep the original packaging until the issue has been reviewed.

Incorrect Items

If you receive a product that is different from the item you ordered, contact us with your order information.

We will work with you to resolve the issue.

Custom & Personalised Orders

Custom and personalised products are made specifically according to the customer's requested design, colours, names, initials or other specifications.

Because of their personalised nature, returns or exchanges may not be available simply because the customer changes their mind.

If there is a problem with a personalised order, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

Change Of Mind

Please choose your products carefully before submitting an order.

Since many items are handmade specifically for an order, a change-of-mind return may not be possible once production has started.

Exchanges

Exchanges are considered on a case-by-case basis depending on the product, its condition and whether it has been personalised.

Contact us before sending anything back. Do not ship an item without receiving instructions first.

Return Conditions

Where a return is approved, the item may need to be unused, in its original condition and accompanied by relevant order details.

Additional conditions may apply depending on the specific product and circumstances.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, the amount and method of the refund will depend on the circumstances of the order and the original payment method.

Any applicable delivery or transaction costs may be considered separately.
