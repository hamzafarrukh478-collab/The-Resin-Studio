Overview

Simple, transparent.

The Resin Studio respects your privacy and aims to handle your information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you interact with the website and how that information may be used.

Last updated: August 2026

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information you provide may include your name, contact details, email address, delivery information and order details.

For custom orders, you may also provide personalised information such as names, initials, colours, design preferences or other details required to create your item.

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to process and communicate about your orders, respond to enquiries, arrange delivery or collection, and create personalised products.

Information may also be used to improve the website and customer experience.

Order Information

When you submit an order or enquiry, the information you provide may be used to identify your order and communicate with you about it.

Order information should only be shared with people or services when reasonably necessary to fulfil the order.

Contact Information

If you contact The Resin Studio by phone, WhatsApp, email or another communication method, the information you provide may be retained as necessary to respond to your request or manage your order.

Cookies & Website Data

The website may use basic browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality.

For example, shopping-cart information may be stored locally in your browser so that products you select can remain in your cart while browsing.

Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services such as web hosting, fonts, analytics, communication platforms or payment-related services.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Payment Information

The Resin Studio does not intend to collect unnecessary sensitive payment information through the website.

If payment is handled through a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed directly by that provider under its applicable policies.

Sharing Information

Personal information is not intended to be sold or publicly disclosed.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to fulfil an order, provide a requested service, operate the website, comply with legal obligations or protect the rights and safety of the studio and its customers.

Information Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by The Resin Studio.

However, no website, online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact The Resin Studio if you have questions about information you have provided or wish to request clarification about how it is being handled.

Requests may be subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, business practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version published on this page will apply to future website interactions.
