Behind The Craft

The Making Process

From the first idea to the final finish, every creation passes through a careful hands-on process.

From Idea To Creation

Nothing rushed.

Resin art requires patience. Every layer, detail and finishing step has its own timing.

That's why each piece is given the attention it needs rather than being rushed through production.

The Journey

Six steps. One piece.

A glimpse into how an idea becomes a finished handmade creation.

01 / IDEA

Imagine

Every piece begins with an idea — a colour combination, a shape, a theme or a customer's vision.

02 / DESIGN

Design

Colours, decorative elements, shapes and personalised details are selected and arranged before the resin is poured.

03 / POUR

Pour

Resin is carefully poured and combined with the chosen decorative elements to create the desired effect.

04 / CURE

Cure

The piece is given time to cure properly. Patience here helps create a strong, finished surface.

05 / FINISH

Refine

Once cured, the piece is checked, refined and finished so that the final result looks polished and ready to use.

06 / READY

Deliver

The completed creation is carefully prepared for collection or delivery and begins its journey to its new owner.

The Handmade Difference

Every detail matters.

Handmade doesn't mean imperfect. It means every piece receives individual attention.

From choosing the colours to checking the final surface, the process is built around creating something that feels personal.

What Goes Into It

Small details, big difference.

01 / MATERIALS

Carefully Chosen

Resin, pigments, decorative elements and finishing materials are selected according to the design of each piece.

02 / PATIENCE

Time Matters

Resin needs time to settle and cure. The process cannot simply be rushed.

03 / FINISH

Final Check

Each finished piece is checked before it is prepared for its new home.
