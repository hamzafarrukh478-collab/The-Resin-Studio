Shipping

Made with care. Sent with care.

Every piece from The Resin Studio is carefully prepared, packed and sent so it reaches you safely and beautifully.

06
Our Promise

From our studio to you.

Because resin pieces can be delicate, we take extra care when preparing every order for delivery.

Orders are checked, protected and packed before they leave the studio. Custom orders may require additional preparation time depending on their design.

The Journey

Your order's journey.

A simple process designed to keep your handmade pieces protected from studio to doorstep.

01 — CONFIRM

Order Confirmed

Once your order details are received, we review the products and confirm the order information with you.

02 — PREPARE

Carefully Prepared

Each piece is checked and prepared before being packed for delivery.

03 — PACK

Securely Packed

Your handmade piece is protected carefully to reduce the risk of damage during transportation.

04 — DELIVER

On Its Way

Your order is handed over for delivery and begins its journey to you.

Information

Before your order ships.

A few things worth knowing before placing your order.

01

Handmade Timing

Many of our pieces are handmade individually. This means preparation time can vary depending on the product and order volume.

02

Custom Orders

Custom pieces may take longer than ready-made items because they are created specifically for you.

03

Packaging

We use protective packaging appropriate for handmade resin products to help keep your order safe during transportation.

Important

Shipping times can vary.

Delivery time depends on the destination, courier and whether your order is ready-made or custom. If you need an order by a specific date, please contact us before placing the order so we can confirm whether the timing is possible.

Need Help?

Questions about delivery?

We're happy to help with questions about your order, custom pieces or delivery arrangements.
