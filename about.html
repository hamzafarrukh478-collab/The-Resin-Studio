Our Story

Made by hand. Made to keep.

The Resin Studio is a small creative studio built around handcrafted resin art, thoughtful details and pieces made to bring something special into everyday life.

06
The Beginning

A little resin, a lot of imagination.

The Resin Studio began from a love for creating beautiful things by hand.

Resin allows every piece to become something different. Colours, shapes, textures and tiny details can come together in ways that make no two handmade pieces exactly alike.

From small personalised keychains and bookmarks to decorative trays, jewellery boxes and larger artwork, every piece is created with patience and attention to detail.

Our Philosophy

Handmade should feel personal.

We don't want our pieces to feel mass-produced. They should feel like they were made for someone.

That's why we love personalised orders, meaningful gifts and designs that have a story behind them.

A small keychain can carry a name. A bookmark can become part of someone's daily routine. A jewellery box can become something treasured for years.

What Matters
