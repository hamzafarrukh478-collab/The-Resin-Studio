FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Tap any question to reveal the answer.

Are all your products handmade?

Yes. Our resin products are handmade individually, which makes every piece unique. Because they are handcrafted, tiny differences between pieces are natural and part of their character.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. We offer custom orders for customers who want something personalised. You can tell us your preferred design, colours, name, shape or theme through our Custom Orders page.

Can I choose my own colours?

For many custom pieces, yes. Colour availability depends on the design and materials required. Contact us with your preferred colours and we can discuss the possibilities.

How long does a custom order take?

Custom orders can take longer than ready-made products because each piece has to be designed and handcrafted. The exact time depends on the size, design and quantity of your order.

Are the colours exactly the same as shown online?

We do our best to photograph our products accurately, but colours can appear slightly different depending on lighting, photography and your screen.

How do I place an order?

Browse our Shop, choose the products you want and add them to your cart. You can then review your order and submit your order details.

Can I order multiple products together?

Yes. You can add multiple products to your cart before submitting your order. This makes it easy to order several handmade pieces together.

How do I pay for my order?

Payment and order arrangements can be confirmed directly with The Resin Studio after submitting your order.

Can I buy a resin piece as a gift?

Of course. Handmade resin pieces can make thoughtful gifts for birthdays, special occasions, friends and family. Customisation can also make your gift more personal.

Why might my handmade piece look slightly different?

Resin is a handmade medium, so every pour can have subtle differences in colour, pattern, bubbles and texture. These variations are normal and help make each piece one of a kind.

How should I care for my resin products?

Keep resin pieces away from excessive heat and prolonged direct sunlight. Clean them gently with a soft, damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials.

Can I request a different size?

Some products can be made in different sizes. Availability depends on the particular design. Contact us with the size you have in mind and we can let you know what is possible.

Do you accept special gift requests?

Yes. If you have a specific idea for a gift, especially a personalised resin piece, you can contact us and discuss your requirements.
